## Series A: Multi-Isotope Hybrid Diamond Batteries

### Model ADB-H1K: Hybrid Alpha-Beta Diamond Core
**Power Output:** 1-10 kW continuous  
**Core Innovation:** Dual-isotope engineered decay system

**Technical Breakthrough:**
Based on research showing nuclear batteries have potential to achieve specific powers of 1–50 mW/g with alpha emitters like Am241 offering higher specific powers, this design combines:

- **Primary Alpha Core:** Americium-241 (half-life: 432 years)
  - Power density: Am241 devices show significantly higher specific power but with reduced operational lifetime
  - Encased in ultra-pure synthetic diamond matrix
  - Alpha particles provide 5.5 MeV energy vs. 156 keV for carbon-14 beta

- **Secondary Beta Enhancement:** Carbon-14 from recycled nuclear waste
  - Provides long-term baseline power as Am-241 decays
  - Extends operational lifetime to 5,000+ years
  - Beta particles fill energy gaps between alpha events

**Advanced Diamond Architecture:**
- **3D Nanostructured Diamond Lattice:** Maximizes surface area for particle capture
- **Boron-Doped Diamond Semiconductors:** Enhanced conductivity and efficiency
- **Layered Isolation Design:** Prevents alpha radiation damage to beta components

**Performance Specifications:**
- Power density: 15-25 mW/g (100x improvement over current designs)
- Conversion efficiency: 12-18% (vs. current 1-6%)
- Operational life: 50+ years at 90% capacity
- Safety: Triple-layer diamond containment, zero external radiation

### Model ADB-H100K: Americium-Tritium Cascade Battery
**Power Output:** 100 kW - 1 MW continuous  
**Innovation:** Cascading decay energy harvesting

**Theoretical Foundation:**
Plutonium-238 must be deliberately produced but can be easily converted into stable plutonium oxide ceramic with reasonable power density of 0.57 watts per gram. This design improves on that concept using more readily available isotopes.

**Dual-Stage Energy Conversion:**
1. **Primary Stage:** Americium-241 decay produces high-energy alphas
2. **Secondary Stage:** Alpha bombardment of Lithium-6 produces tritium
3. **Tertiary Stage:** Tritium beta decay provides sustained power
4. **Quaternary Stage:** Waste heat capture via thermoelectric conversion

**Engineering Specifications:**
- **Reactor Core:** 500kg of carefully engineered Am-241/Li-6 matrix
- **Diamond Shell:** 50-layer synthetic diamond containment system
- **Power Conditioning:** Advanced DC-DC conversion with 95% efficiency
- **Thermal Management:** Integrated heat pipes and radiative cooling
- **Modular Design:** Hot-swappable 10 MW units for scalability

**Safety Innovations:**
- **Neutron Moderation:** Advanced boron-carbide neutron absorbers
- **Fail-Safe Design:** Passive shutdown on containment breach
- **Remote Monitoring:** Real-time isotope tracking and health monitoring
