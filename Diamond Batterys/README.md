# Diamond Batterys — Advanced Energy Storage Research

> **⚡ Overview**: Diamond-based energy storage pushed past press-release depth — materials science, physical limits, and design arguments for diamond anode batteries.

---

## 🔋 Overview

**Diamond Batterys** explores diamond-based energy storage technologies, particularly diamond anode batteries (DABs). This work goes beyond promotional claims to examine the materials science, physical limitations, and realistic design pathways for this emerging technology.

### Key Concepts

- **Diamond Anode Battery**: Using diamond's unique electronic properties for energy storage
- **Conduction Band Engineering**: Tuning diamond's bandgap for ion conduction
- **Electrochemical Stability**: Exploiting diamond's exceptional chemical inertness
- **Thermal Management**: Leveraging diamond's superior thermal conductivity

## 🧪 Materials Considerations

- **Boron Doping**: Achieving p-type conductivity through boron impurities
- **Nitrogen Impurities**: Investigating n-type doping pathways
- **Defect Engineering**: Creating controlled defects for ion intercalation
- **Surface Termination**: Optimizing surface chemistry for electrode reactions

## ⚙️ Electrochemical Processes

- **Lithium Interposition**: Li-ion storage in diamond lattice
- **Sodium Interposition**: Na-ion storage for cost-effective alternatives
- **Charge Transport**: Understanding ion mobility through diamond
- **Reaction Kinetics**: Optimizing charge/discharge rates
