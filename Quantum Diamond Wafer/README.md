# Quantum Diamond Wafer — QDMP and CVD Pathways

> **💎 Overview**: **QDMP** and CVD paths toward quantum-grade diamond wafers — roadmap, not fantasy gloss.

---

## 💎 Overview

**Quantum Diamond Wafer** explores quantum diamond membrane production (QDMP) and chemical vapour deposition (CVD) pathways toward quantum-grade diamond wafers. This work provides a realistic roadmap for producing diamond wafers suitable for quantum computing and sensing applications.

### Key Concepts

- **QDMP**: Quantum Diamond Membrane Production
- **CVD**: Chemical Vapour Deposition growth technique
- **Quantum-Grade**: Diamonds meeting quantum computing requirements
- **Roadmap**: Pragmatic development pathway
