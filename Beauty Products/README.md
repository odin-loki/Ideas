## 💅 Overview

**Beauty Products** explores cosmetic research and development with scientific citations. This work examines real formulations backed by clinical research, ethnobotanical evidence, and peer-reviewed literature.

### Key Concepts

- **Clinical Substantiation**: Every ingredient has supporting research
- **Ethnobotanical Evidence**: Traditional wisdom with modern validation
- **Formulation Science**: Understanding ingredient interactions
- **Citation-Based**: Claims backed by published research
