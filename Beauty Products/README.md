## 🧪 What's actually in the paper

The paper structures the lotion as three coordinated phases:

| Phase | Key ingredients | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Lipid phase** | Hemp seed oil (anchor), prickly pear, sea buckthorn, baobab | Trans-epidermal hydration, barrier reinforcement |
| **Water phase** | *Tremella* (snow mushroom) polysaccharides | Next-generation humectants |
| **Active phase** | Centella asiatica, hibiscus, horsetail, rosehip seed oil | Collagen / elastin synthesis, anti-inflammatory cytokine modulation, antioxidant scavenging |

Five claimed dermatological axes (per the paper's Executive Summary): deep trans-epidermal hydration, barrier reinforcement, collagen / elastin synthesis stimulation, anti-inflammatory cytokine modulation, antioxidant free-radical scavenging.

> Earlier README copy listed additional product lines ("Nootropic Formulations", "Anti-Aging") and "Key Takeaways" — those products **do not exist** as papers in this folder. Only the Hemp Harmony paper is present.
