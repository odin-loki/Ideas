## 🔷 Overview

**Cypha** explores the Cypha/HRNA (Human-RNA Algorithm) universe, including v8 research bundles, older stacks, prototypes, and pattern-matching tooling. This work tells the comprehensive encoder story in depth, from early prototypes through the latest v8 implementation.

### Key Concepts

- **HRNA**: Human-RNA Algorithm concept
- **CyphaDIF**: Cypha Digital Information Format
- **Pattern Matching**: Pattern recognition and matching
- **Encoder Story**: The evolution of the encoder system
