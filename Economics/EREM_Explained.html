⚖️ Why Quality Factors Matter

Not All Energy is Created Equal

1 MJ of nuclear power ≠ 1 MJ of coal. They have different:

  • Energy density - How much energy per kg?
  • Transportability - Can you move it easily?
  • Controllability - Can you turn it on/off when needed?
  • Waste impact - What's left behind?
Energy Source Quality Factor (Q) Why?
Nuclear 1.00 Reference standard. Ultra-dense, controllable, minimal volume waste
Hydroelectric 0.82 Clean, controllable, but location-fixed
Petroleum 0.75 Excellent portability, but CO2 waste
Natural Gas 0.68 Fast response, pipeline infrastructure needed
Wind 0.65 Clean but intermittent, capacity factor ~35%
Solar 0.58 Clean but intermittent, capacity factor ~25%
Coal 0.42 Low density, slow response, heavy pollution

This means: 1 million tonnes of uranium = 2.4 million tonnes of coal in economic value, even though uranium has vastly more joules. The Q-factor captures real-world usefulness.
