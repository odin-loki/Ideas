## 🧪 Common design philosophy

- **Activity-dependent NMDA modulation.** All three compounds use *partial allosteric* NMDA-receptor modulation rather than direct agonism / antagonism, so the effect strength scales with existing receptor activity. This is the structural commitment that lets a single compound improve both positive and negative symptoms.
- **Long-tail neuroplasticity via BDNF.** Each compound is designed to induce sustained BDNF release rather than transient receptor occupancy, with explicit "months per dose" plasticity targets in the design notes.
- **Multi-axis stabilisation.** Dopamine turnover stabilisation + indirect GABA enhancement are designed to be downstream consequences of the primary NMDA effect, not separate pharmacological targets.

## ⚠️ Honest framing and safety

- **All three compounds are explicitly fictional.** Folder name "Schizophrenia Cure" overstates intent: these are *theoretical research compounds* with no human or animal validation.
- **Not medical advice.** Do not synthesise, possess, or administer the compounds described.
- The clinical context (NMDA-hypofunction hypothesis of schizophrenia, BDNF and neuroplasticity literature, dopamine D2 / GABA interactions) is grounded in published research; the *novel compounds* are the fiction.
- Real schizophrenia treatment is multi-modal (antipsychotic medication + psychotherapy + community support) and remains a managed long-term condition; "cure" framing should not be taken at face value.
