## Mechanism of Action

### Primary Target: Adenylyl Cyclase (AC)
Neurofoskin-7 binds to the allosteric site of adenylyl cyclase, specifically targeting AC1 and AC8 isoforms highly expressed in the brain.

### Molecular Cascade
```
NF-7 → Adenylyl Cyclase Activation → ↑cAMP → PKA Activation → 
CREB Phosphorylation → BDNF Gene Expression → Neuroplasticity
```

### Detailed Pathway
1. **Binding**: NF-7 hydroxyl groups form hydrogen bonds with AC active site
2. **Conformational Change**: Stabilizes AC in active configuration
3. **cAMP Production**: 3-5x increase in intracellular cAMP levels
4. **PKA Activation**: cAMP binds regulatory subunits, releases catalytic subunits
5. **Nuclear Signaling**: PKA phosphorylates CREB at Ser133
6. **Gene Transcription**: pCREB binds CRE elements, upregulates neurotrophin genes
7. **Protein Synthesis**: Increased BDNF, NGF, NT-3 production
8. **Neuroplasticity**: Enhanced synaptic strength, dendritic branching, neurogenesis

### Secondary Effects

- **Epigenetic Changes**: CREB-mediated histone modifications
