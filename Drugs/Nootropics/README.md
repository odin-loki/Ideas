## 🧪 Compound design principles (from the source papers)

- **α7 / α4β2 nAChR family.** Cognicline targets the same receptor family as DMXBA / TC-5619 / encenicline but with a partial-agonist + PAM design intended to reduce the rapid desensitisation that defeated prior candidates.
- **Multi-mechanism beats single-target.** CogniMax-Pro is built on the explicit assumption that single-target nootropics have plateaued in this population; the formulation hits five mechanistic pillars in parallel.
- **Mixed real/fictional inventory.** CogniMax-Pro is unusual in pairing real clinically-studied compounds (xanomeline analogue, berberine phytosome, α-GPC, NMN, PQQ, CoQ10) with three explicitly-flagged fictional novel molecules. The paper is honest about which is which.

---

## ⚠️ Honest framing and safety

- **All compound activity is theoretical.** No human or animal data exists for any compound described. The pharmacology is *predicted* from molecular structure, not measured.
- **Not medical advice.** Do not synthesise, possess, or administer the compounds described. Several would fall under controlled-substance frameworks if real.
- The clinical *context* (cognitive deficits in schizophrenia, antipsychotic side-effect profiles, published trial outcomes for prior nicotinic candidates) is real and well-cited; the *novel compounds* added on top are the fiction.
