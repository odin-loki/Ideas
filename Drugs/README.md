## 🧪 Overview

This folder is a research-style exploration of **injectable depot drug delivery** and several speculative compound designs. The papers treat drug delivery as a systems-engineering problem — release kinetics, polymer matrices, phase-inversion gels, dosage scaling, burst-release management — and apply that framework to invented compounds for nutrition, cognition, and psychiatry.

The work is technically literate (real polymers, real release mechanisms, real ICH Q8 / Quality-by-Design framing) but the **specific compounds** discussed are not real drugs and have not undergone any clinical or preclinical validation. The folder is creative worldbuilding with scientific scaffolding.

## 🔬 What Paper 3 (UDS) Actually Contains

The Universal Depot Systems paper is the most technically detailed in the folder. It covers:

- **Four release mechanisms** — dissolution-controlled, diffusion-controlled (Higuchi t^0.5), degradation-controlled (PLGA, PCL, polyanhydrides), osmotic-controlled
- **Polymer systems** — PLGA microspheres, in-situ forming implants (NMP / phase inversion), thermoreversible poloxamer gels, lipid vehicles
- **Duration ladder** — formulation strategies from 2-hour to 12-month targets
- **Drug-class compatibility** — hydrophilic / lipophilic small molecules, large biomolecules
- **Mathematical release modelling** — Higuchi, first-order, zero-order regimes
- **Burst-release minimisation strategies**
- **Quality-by-Design under ICH Q8**
- **Four-phase preclinical-to-clinical workflow**

It is framed against real marketed depots (paliperidone palmitate, aripiprazole monohydrate, leuprolide acetate, medroxyprogesterone acetate, cabotegravir + rilpivirine) — i.e., the depot science is real; the framework is being explained around it.

## 🧠 Research Themes

### Depot delivery

- **Matrix formulation** (PLGA, PCL, polyanhydrides) for controlled hydrolysis-driven release
- **In-situ forming implants** via solvent exchange (NMP carrier)
- **Stimuli-responsive release** triggered by physiological signals
- **Multi-compartment / TriPhase** architectures for layered release profiles

### Injectable nutrition

- **Caloric density** vs. injection volume tradeoffs
- **Absorption profile** across IM / SC / IV routes
- **Stability** under storage and at injection
- **Biocompatibility** and adverse-reaction minimisation

### Nootropic systems

- **Blood–brain barrier penetration** strategies
- **Receptor-selective targeting** (e.g., α7 nAChR for Cognicline)
- **Duration tuning** matched to use case
- **Stacking** for synergistic mechanisms

### Schizophrenia therapeutics (speculative)

- **NMDA partial allosteric modulation** as an activity-dependent regulator
- **BDNF release** for neuroplasticity recovery
- **Self-regulating** (effect strength scales with existing receptor activity)
