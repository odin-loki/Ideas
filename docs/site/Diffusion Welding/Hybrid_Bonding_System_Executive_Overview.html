How It Works

The Innovation: Three Synergistic Mechanisms

1. Electrochemical Ion Migration
- Metal atoms ionize (e.g., Al → Al³⁺)
- Migrate through ionic liquid substrate under electric field
- Deposit at interface (Al³⁺ → Al)
- Activation energy: 20 kJ/mol (7x lower than thermal diffusion)
- Works at room temperature to 300°C

2. Thermal Diffusion
- Traditional solid-state diffusion
- Enhanced by chemical substrate (reduces activation energy 30-45%)
- Activation energy: 78-163 kJ/mol (chemistry-enhanced)
- Dominates at higher temperatures

3. Ultrasonic Assistance
- Mechanical oxide disruption
- Acoustic streaming enhances mass transport
- 2-3x diffusion enhancement
- Powers: 1-15 W/cm² depending on regime

Key Insight: These mechanisms are independent and additive. At low temperature, electrochemical dominates (98%). At high temperature, thermal diffusion takes over. This creates a tunable system.

Technology Components

Chemical Substrate (The Key Innovation)

Replaces the vacuum chamber of traditional diffusion welding:

Standard Formulation:
- 65% Ionic liquid (EMIM-Cl + metal chloride) - ion transport
- 15% Gallium - oxide breakthrough, wetting agent
- 10% Organometallic component - reactive metal delivery
- 5% Catalysts (Cu²⁺, Zn powder) - electrochemical enhancement
- 5% Carrier (ethanol/propylene carbonate) - application viscosity

Functions:
1. Oxide removal (chemical + gallium penetration)
2. Ionic conduction (electrochemical bonding)
3. Reduced activation energy (30-45% decrease)
4. Metal atom source (organometallic + electrochemical)
5. Ultrasonic coupling medium

All components consumed into joint - no residue
