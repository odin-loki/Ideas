BASE PREPARATIONS

Mushroom Elements

  1. Mushroom Stock Base - 200g shiitake mushrooms - 100g porcini mushrooms - 100g king oyster mushrooms - 50g dried forest mushrooms - 200g butter Method: - Sauté mushrooms in butter until golden - Add 1L water - Simmer 2 hours - Strain and clarify - Store chilled

  2. Mushroom Butter-Washed Spirits - Shiitake vodka - Porcini bourbon - Forest mushroom gin Method: - Use strained butter from stock - Fat-wash for 4 hours - Freeze and strain - Fine filter

  3. Mushroom Tinctures - Fresh mushroom blend - High-proof neutral spirit - 7-day infusion - Strain and bottle

Marine Elements

  1. Salmon Oil Preparation - Cold-pressed salmon oil - Filter 3 times - Store in dropper bottles - Max 3 drops per drink - Refresh weekly

  2. Dashi Stock Base - Kombu seaweed - Bonito flakes - Cold brew 24 hours - Clarify and chill - Use within 48 hours

  3. Marine Tincture - Dried seaweed blend - Dried fish powder - High-proof spirit - 5-day infusion

Fermented Elements

  1. Miso Wash - White miso base - Neutral spirit - 2-hour infusion - Filter thoroughly - Use within 1 week

  2. Koji Preparation - Fresh koji rice - Cold water infusion - 12-hour steep - Clarify and chill

  3. Soy Elements - Aged soy sauce reduction - Black vinegar syrup - Store in dropper bottles

PREPARATION NOTES

Storage Guidelines

  1. Mushroom Elements - Stock: 5 days max - Washed spirits: 1 month - Tinctures: 3 months

  2. Marine Elements - Salmon oil: 1 week - Dashi: 48 hours - Tinctures: 2 months

  3. Fermented Elements - Miso wash: 1 week - Koji prep: 3 days - Soy elements: 1 month

Quality Control

  1. Daily Checks - Taste all stocks - Check infusions - Verify aromas - Monitor clarity

  2. Weekly Prep - Fresh mushroom stock - New dashi - Koji preparation - Oil filtration

  3. Monthly Tasks - Deep clean - Start new tinctures - Test all recipes - Staff training

TECHNIQUE SPECIFICS

Clarification Methods

  1. Stock Clarification - Agar clarification - Fine straining - Coffee filter - Final polish

  2. Oil Preparation - Triple filtering - Centrifuge if available - Dropper bottle portioning - Regular testing

Fat-Washing Protocol

  1. Initial Infusion - Room temp - 4 hours maximum - Gentle agitation - Strain while warm

  2. Freezing - Minimum 6 hours - Single large block - Remove fats completely - Fine strain

Safety Protocols

  1. Storage - All items dated - Temperature logs - Clarity checks - Aroma verification

  2. Testing - Daily taste tests - pH monitoring - Visual inspection - Staff verification
