SAVORY CREAM SODA

Cocktails\savory-cream-soda.md

Summary

Method: 1. Let ice cream soften at room temperature 2. Blend with vodka until completely smooth 3. Fine strain through mesh 4. Bottle and refrigerate 5. Shake well before each use Shelf Life: 1 week refrigerated

BASE RECIPE

Core Specifications

  • 40ml Vodka-Ice Cream Mix*
  • 20ml Cointreau
  • 1 teaspoon Black Pepper Brine
  • 3 drops Coriander Essence
  • Soda Water to fill
  • Large Collins Glass
  • Cubed Ice

*Vodka-Ice Cream Mix detailed below

Build Order

  1. Add pepper brine and coriander essence to glass
  2. Add ice to 3/4 full
  3. Pour in vodka-ice cream mix
  4. Add Cointreau
  5. Gentle stir (3-4 rotations)
  6. Top slowly with soda water
  7. Final gentle stir (1-2 rotations)

COMPONENT PREPARATIONS

Vodka-Ice Cream Mix

Yield: 500ml batch - 350ml Premium Vodka (40% ABV) - 150g Vanilla Ice Cream - Pinch of salt

Method: 1. Let ice cream soften at room temperature 2. Blend with vodka until completely smooth 3. Fine strain through mesh 4. Bottle and refrigerate 5. Shake well before each use Shelf Life: 1 week refrigerated

Pepper Brine

Use fresh brine from quality black peppercorn pickle/ferment Alternative: Create quick brine - 100g Black Peppercorns - 500ml Water - 15g Salt Bring to boil, cool, strain, use after 24 hours

Coriander Essence

Quick method: - 50g Fresh Coriander Seeds - 100ml High-proof Neutral Spirit Crush seeds, infuse 48 hours, fine strain

VARIATIONS

Winter Version

  • Replace vanilla ice cream with caramel ice cream
  • Add 2 drops vanilla extract
  • Garnish with fresh cracked pepper

Summer Version

  • Use lemon sorbet instead of ice cream
  • Add 5ml lime juice
  • Garnish with coriander sprig

Non-Alcoholic Version

  • Replace vodka mix with vanilla bean syrup and cream
  • Use orange blossom water instead of Cointreau
  • Increase pepper brine to 2 teaspoons
  • Double soda water

TECHNICAL NOTES

Temperature Control

  • Pre-chill glass
  • Use fresh, dense ice
  • Vodka-ice cream mix must be very cold
  • Soda water should be well-chilled

Texture Management

  • Watch for splitting of dairy
  • Pour soda gently against spoon
  • Don't over-stir
  • Serve immediately

Troubleshooting

If Splitting: - Reduce pepper brine - Add mix before ice - Stir more gently - Check temperature

If Too Sweet: - Increase pepper brine - Reduce Cointreau - Use less sweet ice cream - Add more soda

If Too Savory: - Reduce pepper brine - Add 5ml simple syrup - Increase Cointreau - Use sweeter ice cream

SERVICE NOTES

Glassware

  • Tall Collins Glass
  • Minimum 350ml capacity
  • No garnish required
  • Can use highball alternative

Ice Program

  • Clear cubed ice
  • Fill to 3/4
  • Avoid crushed ice
  • Change if dilution too fast

Visual Presentation

  • Slight cream color
  • Small bubbles
  • No garnish needed
  • Should be slightly cloudy

Flavor Development

  • Initial: Orange and cream
  • Mid: Pepper warmth develops
  • Finish: Herbal coriander notes
  • Length: Medium to long

PAIRING SUGGESTIONS

Food Pairings

  • Light pastries
  • Citrus desserts
  • Savory cookies
  • Spiced nuts

Time of Service

  • Afternoon service
  • Pre-dinner
  • Dessert alternative
  • Summer evenings

Weather Considerations

  • Best served in warm weather
  • Indoor service in winter
  • Adjust ice to temperature
  • Consider seasonal variations
