PREPARATION NOTES

Fat-Washing Technique

  1. Combine spirit with chosen fat (e.g., sandalwood nut butter, argan oil)
  2. Let infuse for 4 hours at room temperature
  3. Freeze until fat solidifies
  4. Strain through cheesecloth
  5. Fine strain through coffee filter

Green Ant Infusion

  1. Add 20 green ants per 500ml gin
  2. Infuse for 2 hours maximum
  3. Strain and bottle
  4. Will keep for 6 months

Native Fruit Processing

  • Kakadu plum: Juice fresh or rehydrate dried
  • Desert lime: Use fresh or frozen, never dried
  • Quandong: Create syrup from fresh or dried fruit
  • Davidson plum: Use fresh juice or reduce to syrup

Garnish Preparation

  • Dehydrated fruits: 57°C for 12 hours
  • Candied herbs: Sugar syrup bath, then dehydrate
  • Crystallized insects: Sugar syrup coat, then dry
  • Smoking herbs: Use fresh sprigs on hot slate

SOURCING NOTES

Australian Native Ingredients

  • Green ants: Northern Territory suppliers
  • Desert lime: South Australian arid regions
  • Kakadu plum: Top End communities
  • Quandong: Central Australian suppliers
  • Sandalwood nut: Western Australian plantations

International Ingredients

  • Source through specialist importers
  • Ensure ethical supply chains
  • Build relationships with native ingredient suppliers
  • Consider seasonal availability

SERVICE NOTES

Glassware Temperature

  • Coupettes: -4°C
  • Nick & Nora: -4°C
  • Rocks glasses: Room temperature
  • Dragon pearl: -2°C

Ice Program

  • Hand-cut blocks for rocks glasses
  • Crushed for builds
  • Quick-shake with standard cubes
  • Long shake with cobbled ice

Workflow Tips

  • Prep all native fruit juices fresh daily
  • Maintain separate bottles for infused spirits
  • Label and date all tinctures and infusions
  • Regular tasting notes for quality control
