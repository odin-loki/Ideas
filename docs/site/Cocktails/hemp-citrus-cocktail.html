HEMP OIL & HONEY COCKTAIL: "GOLDEN FIELDS"

BASE RECIPE

Core Specifications

  • 45ml Rye Whiskey
  • 20ml Yellow Chartreuse
  • 15ml Honey Syrup (3:1)*
  • 3 drops Hemp Oil
  • 2 drops Orange Blossom Water
  • Rocks Glass
  • Large Format Ice

*Honey syrup detailed below

Build Method

  1. Add honey syrup to mixing glass
  2. Add hemp oil
  3. Add rye whiskey
  4. Add Yellow Chartreuse
  5. Add orange blossom water
  6. Add ice
  7. Stir 40 rotations
  8. Strain over large ice cube
  9. Express orange peel and float

COMPONENT PREPARATIONS

Rich Honey Syrup

  • 300g Raw honey
  • 100ml Hot water
    Method:
    1. Combine honey and water
    2. Stir until integrated
    3. Bottle and refrigerate
    4. Use within 2 weeks

Hemp Oil Preparation

  • Store in dark bottle
  • Use dropper only
  • Keep refrigerated
  • Replace monthly
  • Aerate before use

TECHNICAL NOTES

Temperature Control

  • Pre-chill glass
  • Use dense ice
  • Quick service
  • Watch dilution

Balance Points

If Too Sweet:
- Increase rye
- Reduce honey
- Add bitters
- More hemp oil

If Too Savory:
- More Chartreuse
- Increase honey
- Less hemp oil
- Add orange
