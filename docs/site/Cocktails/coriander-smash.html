CORIANDER VODKA TONIC SMASH

Cocktails\coriander-smash.md

Summary

Too Herbal: - Reduce coriander - Increase lime - More tonic - Less muddle time

BASE RECIPE

Core Specifications

  • 45ml Premium Vodka
  • 15-20 Fresh Coriander Leaves
  • 2 Fresh Coriander Stems
  • 3 Lime Wedges
  • Premium Tonic Water
  • Highball Glass
  • Crushed Ice

Build Method

  1. Add coriander leaves and stems to shaker
  2. Add lime wedges
  3. Gentle muddle (4-5 presses)
  4. Add vodka
  5. Short shake with ice
  6. Fine strain into ice-filled glass
  7. Top with tonic (slow pour)
  8. Gentle stir to integrate

COMPONENT DETAILS

Fresh Coriander Prep

  • Use whole plant including stems
  • Wash and dry thoroughly
  • Keep roots for garnish option
  • Store in damp paper towel
  • Replace daily

Ice Program

  • Crushed ice preferred
  • Alternative: Cubed ice
  • Fill glass 3/4
  • Top up as needed

Tonic Selection

  1. Premium Options: - Fever-Tree Indian Tonic - Q Tonic - East Imperial Burma Tonic
  2. Key Qualities: - High quinine content - Natural sweetness - Strong carbonation - Clean finish

VARIATIONS

Spicy Version

  • Add 2 thin slices fresh ginger
  • Include 1 Thai chili
  • Reduce coriander slightly
  • Garnish with chili

Eastern Market

  • Add 3 cucumber slices
  • Include mint leaves
  • Use makrut lime
  • Garnish with cucumber ribbon

Low-ABV Version

  • Reduce vodka to 30ml
  • Add 15ml dry vermouth
  • Increase tonic
  • More herbs

TECHNICAL NOTES

Muddling Technique

  • Gentle pressure only
  • Break cells, don't pulverize
  • Count: 4-5 presses maximum
  • Check aroma release

Temperature Control

  • Pre-chill glass
  • Use fresh ice
  • Chill tonic thoroughly
  • Serve immediately

Balance Points

Too Herbal: - Reduce coriander - Increase lime - More tonic - Less muddle time

Too Weak: - More vodka - Less ice - Shorter tonic pour - More herbs

SERVICE SPECIFICATIONS

Glassware

  • Tall Highball Glass
  • 350-400ml capacity
  • Pre-chilled
  • Clean lines

Visual Presentation

  • Pale green tint
  • Fine herb particles
  • Effervescent
  • Clean ice

Flavor Development

  1. Initial: - Fresh herbs - Citrus burst
  2. Mid-palate: - Vodka cleanness - Tonic bite
  3. Finish: - Lingering herbs - Quinine length

GARNISH OPTIONS

Standard

  • Fresh coriander sprig
  • Lime wheel
  • Both together

Advanced

  1. Coriander Crown: - Full sprig - Root attached - Placed upright
  2. Lime Variation: - Dehydrated wheel - Fresh zest - Twisted peel

SERVICE NOTES

Optimal Service

  • Summer months
  • Afternoon service
  • Pre-dinner
  • Garden settings

Food Pairings

  • Thai cuisine
  • Fresh seafood
  • Light salads
  • Spicy dishes

BATCHING NOTES

Small Batch (10 drinks)

  • 450ml vodka
  • Prep herbs per drink
  • Batch lime juice
  • Keep tonic separate

Large Format (Party Serve)

  1. Pre-batch base: - 450ml vodka - Coriander-infused - Lime juice added
  2. To serve: - 60ml base - Top with tonic - Fresh herbs
