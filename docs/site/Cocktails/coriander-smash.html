CORIANDER VODKA TONIC SMASH

BASE RECIPE

Core Specifications

  • 45ml Premium Vodka
  • 15-20 Fresh Coriander Leaves
  • 2 Fresh Coriander Stems
  • 3 Lime Wedges
  • Premium Tonic Water
  • Highball Glass
  • Crushed Ice

Build Method

  1. Add coriander leaves and stems to shaker
  2. Add lime wedges
  3. Gentle muddle (4-5 presses)
  4. Add vodka
  5. Short shake with ice
  6. Fine strain into ice-filled glass
  7. Top with tonic (slow pour)
  8. Gentle stir to integrate

COMPONENT DETAILS

Fresh Coriander Prep

  • Use whole plant including stems
  • Wash and dry thoroughly
  • Keep roots for garnish option
  • Store in damp paper towel
  • Replace daily

Ice Program

  • Crushed ice preferred
  • Alternative: Cubed ice
  • Fill glass 3/4
  • Top up as needed

Tonic Selection

  1. Premium Options:
    - Fever-Tree Indian Tonic
    - Q Tonic
    - East Imperial Burma Tonic
  2. Key Qualities:
    - High quinine content
    - Natural sweetness
    - Strong carbonation
    - Clean finish

VARIATIONS

Spicy Version

  • Add 2 thin slices fresh ginger
  • Include 1 Thai chili
  • Reduce coriander slightly
  • Garnish with chili

Eastern Market

  • Add 3 cucumber slices
  • Include mint leaves
  • Use makrut lime
  • Garnish with cucumber ribbon

Low-ABV Version

  • Reduce vodka to 30ml
  • Add 15ml dry vermouth
  • Increase tonic
  • More herbs

TECHNICAL NOTES

Muddling Technique

  • Gentle pressure only
  • Break cells, don't pulverize
  • Count: 4-5 presses maximum
  • Check aroma release

Temperature Control

  • Pre-chill glass
  • Use fresh ice
  • Chill tonic thoroughly
  • Serve immediately

Balance Points

Too Herbal:
- Reduce coriander
- Increase lime
- More tonic
- Less muddle time

Too Weak:
- More vodka
- Less ice
- Shorter tonic pour
- More herbs
