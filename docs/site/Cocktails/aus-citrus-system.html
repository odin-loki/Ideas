PREPARATION TECHNIQUES

Juice Extraction

  1. Finger Limes
    - Pearl extraction
    - Gentle pressure
    - Separate by color
    - Store in juice

  2. Desert Limes
    - Quick press
    - Fine strain
    - pH test
    - Vacuum seal

  3. Blood Limes
    - Segment first
    - Gentle press
    - Color preservation
    - Rapid chill

Oil Extraction

  1. Cold Process
    - Muddle with sugar
    - Room temp rest
    - Strain off oils
    - Filter clear

  2. Hot Process
    - Sous vide
    - Low temp
    - Quick chill
    - Fine strain

Preservation Methods

  1. Cordials
    - Add citric acid
    - Light sugar
    - Bottle sterile
    - Refrigerate

  2. Oils
    - Vacuum seal
    - Dark bottles
    - Cool storage
    - Regular testing

USAGE GUIDELINES

Building Drinks

  1. Order of Operations
    - Oils first
    - Fresh juice
    - Syrups/cordials
    - Garnish last

  2. Proportions
    - Max 1oz native citrus
    - Balance with sugar
    - Consider dilution
    - Taste test

Storage Times

  1. Fresh Elements
    - Juice: 24 hours
    - Pearls: 48 hours
    - Zest: Use fresh
    - Segments: 24 hours

  2. Preparations
    - Cordials: 2 weeks
    - Oils: 1 month
    - Tinctures: 3 months
    - Bitters: 6 months

Quality Control

  1. Daily Checks
    - Taste test all juice
    - Check pearls
    - Verify cordials
    - Test drinks

  2. Weekly Tasks
    - Fresh extractions
    - New cordials
    - Oil production
    - Deep clean
