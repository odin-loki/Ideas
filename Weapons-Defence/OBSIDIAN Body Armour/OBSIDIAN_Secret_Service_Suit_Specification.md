## Honest framing

- **Hypothetical / theoretical design.** OBSIDIAN is a paper study with no prototype, no V50 test, no NIJ-IIIA/RF certification, no field trial, and no fielded sponsor. The 11.8 kg total system weight is a budget number, not a measured fielded mass.
- **".50 caliber resistance" at 11.8 kg torso-only is physically unsupportable.** Current best-in-class hard armour defeats .50 BMG (12.7 × 99 mm) at the 12+ kg per-plate class (single front plate), not at 11.8 kg distributed across an entire torso-and-undersuit system. The §"Eternal Ballistic Performance" claim of "Theoretical resistance to all small arms up to .50 caliber" is the single most significant overclaim in the document and should be read as a design aspiration rather than a tested figure. Realistic protection at this weight class is NIJ Level IIIA (handgun) to Level III (5.56 / 7.62 ball) with rifle-rated trauma plates.
- **Material claims are speculative.** Bulk-production carbyne (the stated 6 400+ atom one-dimensional carbon chain) does not exist at engineering scale; large-area graphene + PTFE laminates and macroscopic NiTi shape-memory self-healing matrices at the volumes specified are pre-commercial. The 100+ multi-hit and <10 mm BFD numbers inherit that uncertainty.
- **Concealment trade-off not modelled.** The document combines a 4.2 kg fibre-laminate base, 3.6 kg of hard plates, 2.1 kg of smart textile, and a 4.1 kg distributed exoskeleton — a 14 kg load before footwear (the stated 11.8 kg "total system" already excludes the exoskeleton and footwear). Under a formal suit jacket the resulting drape, profile, and gait signature have not been studied.
- **Single source of truth for numbers.** The portfolio Tier-2 simulator does NOT cover OBSIDIAN ballistic, thermal, or chemical-resistance numbers. Where a number appears in both the simulator and this document, the simulator is authoritative; most OBSIDIAN numbers are document-internal only.
- **Manufacturing / regulatory caveats.** Production at the cited $40 M per-suit cost assumes the speculative materials become commercially producible and that GMP-style quality assurance is in place for the integrated exoskeleton, biometric sensors, and CBRN seal stack — none of which is currently within sovereign Australian manufacturing capability.

### A.1 Ballistic V50 — Lambert–Jonas penetration resistance (conceptual correlation)

> **HYPOTHETICAL DESIGN STUDY — NOT FOR MANUFACTURE**

Resistance to projectile defeat is commonly correlated with armour areal-density through a Lambert–Jonas-type power law on projectile limit velocity.

```
V₅₀ = a · (ρₜ · t)ᵇ
```

- **V₅₀** — ballistic limit velocity (m/s): impact speed at which the armour is perforated ≈50 % of the time (paired with BFD when stopped).
- **ρₜ**, **t** — effective armour material density (kg/m³ or g/cm³, consistent units) and effective thickness along the ballistic normal (m or mm).
- **a**, **b** — empirical constants set by panel construction, projectile class, boundary conditions; **portfolio calibration**: tabulated defeat/stop outcomes and reference V₅₀ values vs standard threats appear in **`weapons_sim_results.md` §13** for soft IIIA, NIJ III, NIJ IV, APES military, and APES-L police stacks.

Example interpretive anchors from §13 (simulator-derived, not OBSIDIAN-specific):

| Armour class (simulator §13 row) | Example threat | Reference V₅₀ (§13 tabulation) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft IIIA (5.5 kg/m²) | 12.7 × 99 M2 AP | 81.0 m/s |
| NIJ IV (25 kg/m²) | 12.7 × 99 M2 AP | 493.0 m/s |

### A.2 Back-face deformation (BFD)

> **HYPOTHETICAL DESIGN STUDY — NOT FOR MANUFACTURE**

When a projectile is stopped, kinetic energy coupling into the armour–witness system produces observable back-face deformation. A simple proportional **excess‑velocity** form is useful for sensitivities (coefficients geometry-dependent):

```
BFD = α · (V_impact − V₅₀)^β       (for stopped threats; qualitative / fitting form)
```

- **α**, **β** — empirically fit for a given armour + threat + backing; **must be calibrated** against clay-witness or instrumentation.
- **V_impact**, **V₅₀** — impact speed and ballistic limit defined as in §A.1.

**Compliance reference:** NIJ 0101.06-style ceramic testing associates **witness BFD < 44 mm** with survivable blunt trauma envelopes for rifle threats (see [`weapons_sim_results.md`](weapons_sim_results.md) §13 BFD column for simulator-reported BFD at limit conditions).

### A.3 Radioisotope power decay (conceptual RTG electrical output)

> **HYPOTHETICAL DESIGN STUDY — NOT FOR MANUFACTURE**

Portfolio sibling studies (e.g. **OBSIDIAN-X**) credit a **hypothetical ~10 W electrical** nuclear-battery subsystem for uninterrupted power. Electrical output **decays** with isotope decay:

```
P_RTG(t) = P₀ · exp(−λ · t)

λ = ln(2) / T_half
```

- **P₀** — initial electrical output (W); **t** — time in **years** if λ is per year.
- **Pu‑238** (typical space / design-study surrogate for long-life α RTGs): **T_half = 87.7 yr** → **λ = ln(2) / 87.7 ≈ 0.0079 yr⁻¹**.

At **t = 20 yr** (notional concealed-system service horizon) with **P₀ = 10 W**:

```
P_RTG(20 yr) = 10 W × exp(−0.0079 × 20) = 10 W × exp(−0.158) ≈ 8.54 W
```

so **≈14 % cumulative decay** (~86 % retained power).

**Thermoelectric inventory (order-of-magnitude).** Classic flight RTG stacks are often quoted near **η_TE ≈ 7–8 %** electrical extraction from delivered thermal power. For a **steady 10 W electrical** output, **P_th = P_el / η_TE ≈ 125–143 W** — *not* the lower thermal bracket. The **~37–50 W thermal** figure sometimes appears in **partial-duty** or **peak-not-average** side discussions; it couples to only **~3–4 W electrical** at **η_TE = 7–8 %**. **If** a design study nevertheless credits **10 W electrical** at **7–8 %**, the honest thermal load is **O(10² W)** and drives **multi‑kg** isotope plus sink mass. Order-of-magnitude **~3–4 kg Pu‑238‑class** inventory scales track open literature on **~100 W‑class** compact sources — still incompatible with low-mass formal concealment.

**Fuel cost implication (illustrative).** Order-of-magnitude marginal **Pu‑238 economics** (~**US $8 000 / g** in high-level programmes, order **10³–10⁴ US $/mg** regimes in public summaries) imply **~(3–4 kg) ⇒ ~US $24–32 M radioisotope cost alone** **before** thermoelectrics, shielding, and qualification. That cost structure matches why sibling **OBSIDIAN-X** unit-economics discussions land on **≈ US $135 M / suit** tiers when speculative power and metamaterial subsets are aggregated (see [`../OBSIDIAN-X Body Armour/OBSIDIAN_X_Specification.md`](../OBSIDIAN-X%20Body%20Armour/OBSIDIAN_X_Specification.md) §XIII / §XVI RTG-premium modelling). **OBSIDIAN (this file)** cites a different illustrative **$40 M** complete-system tabletop number in §Cost Analysis — the RTG decomposition here explains **Pu‑238‑dominant** sensitivities compatible with **higher-tier** OBSIDIAN-X numbering; it does not assert fielded costing.

### A.4 Exosuit joint torque (powered joint claim)

> **HYPOTHETICAL DESIGN STUDY — NOT FOR MANUFACTURE**

This specification's **§Integrated Exoskeleton** describes distributed **shoulder/arm/hip/leg actuators**. For conceptual dynamics of a rigid driven limb segment:

```
τ_joint = I · α + m_limb · g · r_COM
```

- **τ_joint** — required joint torque (N·m).
- **I**, **α** — segment rotational inertia about the joint axis (kg·m²), commanded angular acceleration (rad/s²).
- **m_limb** — limb mass carried by that joint hierarchy (kg); **g** — gravity (≈ 9.81 m/s²); **r_COM** — horizontal moment arm from joint to limb centre of mass (m).

If the armour were redesigned as **passive load carriage only** with no powered torque sources, §A.4 collapses to static posture/balance modelling and **this term is omitted** (**passive system — no powered joints**).

### A.5 Thermal regulation — phase-change buffering (PCM)

> **HYPOTHETICAL DESIGN STUDY — NOT FOR MANUFACTURE**

Same latent-heat bookkeeping as cited for **APES**-family specifications: absorbed heat during fusion soaks transient metabolic / environmental spikes.

```
Q_PCM = m_PCM · ΔH_fus
```

- **Q_PCM** — energy absorbed (J) while solid PCM transitions at **nearly constant temperature**.
- **m_PCM** — encapsulated PCM mass (kg); **ΔH_fus** — specific latent heat of fusion (J/kg) for the chosen salt hydrate / alkane PCM.
