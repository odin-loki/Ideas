# TACT-1 Mark II — 40-Variant Flavour Catalogue

**TRP-2026-021 (companion to spec) — UNCLASSIFIED / FOR OFFICIAL USE ONLY**
**Flavour Development Catalogue · Rev 1 · Natural Ingredients Only · Fixed Nutrient Profile · Common TGase Matrix**

> **Forty natural-ingredient flavour variants of the TACT-1 Mark II tactical compact ration, organised across seven flavour families (Chocolate, Coffee & Tea, Nut & Seed, Fruit, Spice & Sweet, Savoury, Floral & Botanical). Every variant shares the identical TGase-crosslinked micellar-casein + HBCD base matrix, the identical MCT + macadamia + coconut fat phase, the identical NutriComplete-P micronutrient premix, and the identical 95:5 carnauba-wax + shellac compound coating. Flavour additions are 0.5–3 g micro-doses of freeze-dried fruit powders, spice powders, nut flours, or natural extracts blended into the dry powder phase at Step 3 of manufacture; coating variants change the suspended powder colourant in the carnauba layer. The TGase incubation, extrusion temperature, micronutrient load, and macro nutrient profile are unchanged across all forty variants.**

---

All 40 variants share an identical nutrient profile. Flavour additions are at micro-dosage levels (0.5–3 g per bar maximum) and do not materially alter macros, micronutrients, or caloric density. The TGase-crosslinked matrix, fat binder phase, and carnauba coating are unchanged across all variants.

**Flavour addition method.** Natural flavour powders (freeze-dried fruit, spice, cocoa, matcha, etc.) and food-grade extracts are blended into the dry powder phase at Step 3 of manufacture (see spec §8). Coating variants change the carnauba wax outer layer — dark cocoa, white cocoa, natural tints from fruit / vegetable powders. The TGase incubation (Step 2) and extrusion (Step 5) are identical for all variants.

**Complete catalogue at a glance** — the full forty-variant list is grouped by family below. Each variant lists its ID, name, flavour additions (with percentage of bar weight), and coating variant.

## §10 Operational Notes

- **Palate-fatigue rotation.** For extended operations (> 72 hr) a mixed-family pack (e.g. 2× chocolate, 2× fruit, 1× savoury for a 5-bar day) avoids the palate fatigue that single-flavour packs produce.
- **Coating-tint identification.** The carnauba coating tint serves as a tactile / visual identifier of family at low light — important when bars are picked from a kit pocket without unwrapping the foil. Dark cocoa = chocolate family; matcha green = matcha; tinted white = fruit / floral; natural dark = savoury.
- **Allergens.** Variants 04, 08, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 26, 28, 38, 40 contain tree nuts, peanuts, or sesame. The flavour catalogue should be reduced to an allergen-free subset (~25 variants) for any operator-specific kit-out where allergen exposure is restricted.
