# Project OBSIDIAN-X — full-body combat armour (hypothetical)

> **A hypothetical 18.5 kg distributed full-body combat-armour study:** ultra-carbyne nanorope coverall, topological-insulator weave, programmable metamaterial fibres, CBRN sealing, HUD helmet, and armoured boots. Design claims include 7.62 mm AP defeat, 50+ multi-hit capability, and −60 °C to +60 °C / 100 m underwater / 8 000 m altitude operation — all **document-internal targets, not simulator-validated.**

> **Genre note.** Hypothetical / pre-physical-test. No prototype, no V50 test, no field trial. Does **not** stop .50 BMG. Nuclear-waste battery and active cloaking claims are order-of-magnitude over-claimed vs published physics.

## 🚧 Honest framing

- **Material claims are speculative** — bulk carbyne, metamaterial cloaking, Cs-137/Co-60 batteries at 10 W are not demonstrated at operational scale.
- **Does not defeat .50 BMG** — design envelope is 7.62 mm AP class.
- **Predecessor:** OBSIDIAN torso-only VIP suit (~11.8 kg).
