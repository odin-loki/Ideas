# Weapons — defence-tech R&D portfolio

> **An honest defence-engineering portfolio, not "fictional weapons concepts".** Source documents are written in the format of formal defence research — *Advanced Defence Systems Research Division*, March 2026, *Classification: UNCLASSIFIED / FOR OFFICIAL USE ONLY* — covering small-arms cartridges, anti-materiel platforms, autocannons, tank rounds, body armour, CBRN protection, hearing protection, energy nutrition, military doctrine, and field-deployable joining technology. Speculative items (combat drug, injectable food) are flagged separately and mirrored from `../Drugs/` for cross-discipline reading.

---

## 🛡 What this folder is

A coordinated portfolio of small-arms / armour / sensor / materiel research, structured as paired **specification sheets** (top-level files in this folder) and **technical research papers** (`Research Papers/`). Two specialised subfolders cover Tactical Acoustic Cancellation (TACS) hearing-protection and rubber-track materiel.

> **TRP-style designators.** Designators like *MAS-15.2E*, *MP-4.6M*, *MP-6.8*, *NACS*, *TACS*, *OBSIDIAN-X* follow defence-portfolio naming conventions. They are research designators inside this portfolio, not fielded systems.

## 🧠 Style and provenance of this portfolio

Source documents are written in formal defence-research register and bear consistent house markers:

- **Authoring division:** *Advanced Defence Systems Research Division* (the recurring brand on each paper).
- **Date stamp:** March 2026 across multiple papers.
- **Classification stamp:** *Classification: UNCLASSIFIED / FOR OFFICIAL USE ONLY*.
- **Australian Defence Force–style framing:** procurement, doctrinal, and capability-gap arguments are written in ADF-style memo prose; designators follow TRP-style conventions.
- **Engineering grounding:** ballistic, materials, and energy analyses are derived from real cited literature where possible (e.g. anti-materiel rifle history from T-Gewehr through Barrett M82A1; comparative tabulations against fielded calibres).

This is best read as a **coordinated R&D portfolio** in the spirit of an internal capability-development study — comparable in form to a defence-procurement research deliverable.

## ⚠️ Honest framing and safety

- **None of these systems exist as fielded matériel.** They are a research portfolio inside this repository. Designators (MAS-15.2E, MP-6.8, NACS, TACS, OBSIDIAN-X, etc.) are internal to the portfolio.
- **Speculative pharma is flagged separately.** Combat Drug and Injectable Food carry a "speculative formulation; not medical advice" disclaimer in their canonical (`Drugs/`) form; the defence framing here re-uses that material under operational doctrine. Neither has any clinical or preclinical validation.
- **Not a build guide and not procurement.** The papers are technical research; reproducing the systems described would in many cases be illegal under firearms / explosives / pharmaceutical / dual-use legislation. Treat as research literature, not as instructions.
- **No actual classification.** The "UNCLASSIFIED / FOR OFFICIAL USE ONLY" header is a *stylistic device* matching the document register; this repository contains no real classified material and no official sponsorship is implied.
