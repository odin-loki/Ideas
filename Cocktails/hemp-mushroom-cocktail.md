# HEMP & MUSHROOM COCKTAIL: "FOREST FLOOR"

## BASE RECIPE

### Core Specifications
- 45ml Mushroom-Washed Vodka*
- 3 drops Hemp Seed Oil
- 15ml Dry Vermouth
- 10ml Mushroom Stock Reduction**
- Pinch of Sea Salt
- Nick & Nora Glass
- No ice in final serve

*Mushroom wash detailed below
**Stock reduction detailed below

### Build Method
1. Add all ingredients to mixing glass
2. Add ice
3. Stir exactly 45 times
4. Rest 10 seconds
5. Strain into chilled glass
6. Express lemon peel and discard
7. Drop of hemp oil on top

## COMPONENT PREPARATIONS

### Mushroom-Washed Vodka (1L Batch)
- 1L Premium Vodka
- 200g Mixed Mushrooms (Shiitake, Porcini, Button)
- 50g Dried Mushrooms
Method:
1. Sauté mushrooms until golden
2. Cool completely
3. Add to vodka
4. Infuse 24 hours
5. Freeze and strain
6. Fine filter

### Mushroom Stock Reduction
- 500g Mixed Mushrooms
- 100g Dried Mushrooms
- 1L Water
- 10g Salt
Method:
1. Simmer 2 hours
2. Strain
3. Reduce to 200ml
4. Clarify
5. Bottle and refrigerate

## TECHNICAL NOTES

### Oil Integration
1. Hemp Oil Handling:
- Store in dark bottle
- Refrigerate
- Use dropper only
- Replace monthly

2. Technique:
- Add last
- Single drop garnish
- Don't over-stir
- Watch temperature

### Temperature Control
- Pre-chill glass
- Use dense ice
- Quick service
- Cold ingredients

### Balance Points
If Too Earthy:
- Reduce mushroom stock
- Add vermouth
- More salt
- Lemon oil

If Too Light:
- More hemp oil
- Increase stock
- Less dilution
- Add umami
