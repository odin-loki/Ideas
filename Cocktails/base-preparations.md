# CORE INGREDIENT SYSTEM

## PRIMARY INFUSIONS (Building Blocks)

### Native Herb Blend #1 "Coastal Essence"
- River mint (40%)
- Sea parsley (30%)
- Native thyme (20%)
- Saltbush (10%)
Usage: Base for infusions, tinctures, and syrups

### Fruit Blend #1 "Bush Orchard"
- Riberry (30%)
- Muntries (30%)
- Native raspberry (20%)
- Bush tomato (20%)
Usage: Base for syrups, shrubs, and cordials

### Spice Blend #1 "Outback Heat"
- Aniseed myrtle (35%)
- Native pepper (35%)
- Strawberry gum (20%)
- Lemon myrtle (10%)
Usage: Tinctures, bitters, rim blends

### Nut Blend #1 "Native Forest"
- Macadamia (40%)
- Bunya nut (30%)
- Wattleseed (20%)
- Native almond (10%)
Usage: Orgeat, fat-washing, garnishes

## SECONDARY PREPARATIONS

### Spirit Infusions
1. Coastal Gin
   - London Dry Gin base
   - Coastal Essence blend infusion
   - 48hr infusion time

2. Bush Vodka
   - Premium vodka base
   - Bush Orchard blend infusion
   - 24hr infusion time

3. Spiced Rum
   - White rum base
   - Outback Heat blend infusion
   - 72hr infusion time

### Syrups (1:1 ratio unless specified)

1. Bush Orchard Syrup
   - Fruit Blend #1
   - Raw sugar
   - 24hr cold infusion

2. Spiced Forest Syrup
   - Spice Blend #1
   - Demerara sugar (2:1)
   - Hot process

3. Native Orgeat
   - Nut Blend #1
   - Raw sugar
   - Orange blossom water
   - 3-day process

3. Bush Orchard Bitters
   - 14-day infusion
