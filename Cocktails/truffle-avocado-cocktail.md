# TRUFFLE & AVOCADO COCKTAIL: "THE EARTH & GROVE"

## BASE RECIPE

### Core Specifications
- 40ml Premium Vodka
- 20ml Cointreau
- 30ml Fresh Avocado Purée*
- 3 drops Truffle Oil**
- Pinch of Maldon Salt
- Coupette Glass
- No ice in final serve

*Fresh avocado preparation detailed below
**Use real truffle oil, not synthetic

### Build Method
1. Add avocado purée to shaker
2. Add vodka and Cointreau
3. Add truffle oil
4. Add pinch of salt
5. Dry shake hard (15 seconds)
6. Add ice
7. Shake again very hard (12 seconds)
8. Double strain into chilled coupette

## COMPONENT PREPARATIONS

### Avocado Purée
For 10 serves:
- 2 ripe avocados
- 15ml lime juice
- Pinch of salt
Method:
1. Blend until completely smooth
2. Pass through fine sieve
3. Vacuum seal if possible
4. Use immediately

### Truffle Component Options
1. Premium:
   - Fresh truffle microplane
   - Real truffle oil
   - Truffle honey drizzle
2. Standard:
   - Quality truffle oil
   - Truffle salt rim
   - Truffle bitters

## TECHNICAL NOTES

### Temperature Control
- Everything must be very cold
- Pre-chill all ingredients
- Double shake essential
- Serve immediately

### Texture Management
1. Achieving Smoothness:
   - Perfect avocado ripeness
   - Thorough blending
   - Hard shaking
   - Fine straining

2. Preventing Oxidation:
   - Vacuum seal purée
   - Fresh preparation
   - Citrus addition
   - Immediate service

### Troubleshooting
If Too Rich:
- Reduce avocado
- Increase vodka
- Add citrus
- More salt

If Too Subtle:
- More truffle
- Less Cointreau
- Add salt
- Increase avocado
