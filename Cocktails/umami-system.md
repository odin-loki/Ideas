# UMAMI FORWARD COCKTAIL SYSTEM

## BASE PREPARATIONS

### Mushroom Elements

1. Mushroom Stock Base
- 200g shiitake mushrooms
- 100g porcini mushrooms
- 100g king oyster mushrooms
- 50g dried forest mushrooms
- 200g butter
Method:
- Sauté mushrooms in butter until golden
- Add 1L water
- Simmer 2 hours
- Strain and clarify
- Store chilled

2. Mushroom Butter-Washed Spirits
- Shiitake vodka
- Porcini bourbon
- Forest mushroom gin
Method:
- Use strained butter from stock
- Fat-wash for 4 hours
- Freeze and strain
- Fine filter

3. Mushroom Tinctures
- Fresh mushroom blend
- High-proof neutral spirit
- 7-day infusion
- Strain and bottle

### Marine Elements

1. Salmon Oil Preparation
- Cold-pressed salmon oil
- Filter 3 times
- Store in dropper bottles
- Max 3 drops per drink
- Refresh weekly

2. Dashi Stock Base
- Kombu seaweed
- Bonito flakes
- Cold brew 24 hours
- Clarify and chill
- Use within 48 hours

3. Marine Tincture
- Dried seaweed blend
- Dried fish powder
- High-proof spirit
- 5-day infusion

### Fermented Elements

1. Miso Wash
- White miso base
- Neutral spirit
- 2-hour infusion
- Filter thoroughly
- Use within 1 week

2. Koji Preparation
- Fresh koji rice
- Cold water infusion
- 12-hour steep
- Clarify and chill

3. Soy Elements
- Aged soy sauce reduction
- Black vinegar syrup
- Store in dropper bottles

2. Oil Preparation

- Dropper bottle portioning

### Fat-Washing Protocol
1. Initial Infusion
- Room temp
- 4 hours maximum
- Gentle agitation
- Strain while warm

2. Freezing
- Minimum 6 hours
- Single large block
- Remove fats completely
- Fine strain
