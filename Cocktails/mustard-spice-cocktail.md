# MUSTARD & NUTMEG COCKTAIL: "THE SPICE MERCHANT"

## BASE RECIPE

### Core Specifications
- 1 teaspoon whole mustard seeds
- 3 drops nutmeg essence
- 45ml aged rum* (adds caramel sweetness to balance mustard)
- 15ml honey liqueur (bridges spice and sweet)
- Old Fashioned Glass
- Large Format Ice

*Suggested: Aged Dominican or Jamaican Rum

### Build Method
1. Muddle mustard seeds gently in glass
2. Add nutmeg essence
3. Add large format ice
4. Pour in rum
5. Add honey liqueur
6. Stir 20 rotations
7. Express orange peel and discard

## COMPONENT PREPARATIONS

### Mustard Seed Preparation
Pre-service prep:
- Toast mustard seeds lightly
- Cool completely
- Store in airtight container
- Replace weekly
Note: Seeds should pop slightly when muddled

### Nutmeg Essence
Quick method:
- 50g Fresh Nutmeg, roughly crushed
- 100ml High-proof Neutral Spirit
Infuse 72 hours, fine strain, store in dropper bottle

### Alternative Build: Pre-Batch Method
For volume service:
1. Crush mustard seeds
2. Infuse in rum for 4 hours
3. Fine strain
4. Add nutmeg essence
5. Bottle and label
Shelf life: 1 month

## VARIATIONS

### Winter Warmer
- Add 10ml allspice dram
- Reduce honey liqueur to 10ml
- Serve warm
- Garnish with star anise

### Summer Spritz
- Add prosecco float
- Reduce rum to 30ml
- Add orange twist
- Serve over crushed ice

### Non-Alcoholic Version
- Replace rum with black tea
- Use honey syrup
- Add orange juice
- Lengthen with ginger beer

## TECHNICAL NOTES

### Muddling Technique
- Gentle pressure only
- Seeds should split, not pulverize
- Remove any broken shells
- Count: 4-5 presses maximum

### Temperature Control
- Glass: Room temperature
- Ice: Straight from freezer
- Spirits: Room temperature
- Final temp: Very cold

### Balance Points
Too Spicy:
- Increase honey liqueur
- Add orange twist
- Reduce mustard seeds
- Extra dilution

Too Sweet:
- Add more mustard
- Reduce honey liqueur
- Extra nutmeg
- Less dilution

## SERVICE SPECIFICATIONS

### Glassware
- Old Fashioned/Rocks Glass
- 250-300ml capacity
- No pre-chill
- Thick base for muddling

### Ice Program
- Single large cube/sphere
- Alternative: 2x2 cubes
- Must be dense/clear
- No crushed ice

### Visual Presentation
- Golden amber color
- Floating mustard seeds
- No garnish needed
- Should be clear

### Flavor Development
1. Initial:
   - Sweet honey notes
   - Rum warmth
2. Mid-palate:
   - Mustard spice develops
   - Nutmeg emerges
3. Finish:
   - Long spice tail
   - Honey returns

## INSPIRATION NOTES

This cocktail draws from:
- Classical spice trade routes
- Traditional mustard preparation
- Rum's historical spice connections
- Ancient nutmeg commerce

The combination creates a unique bridge between:
- Savory (mustard) and sweet (honey)
- Warm (nutmeg) and sharp (mustard)
- Rich (rum) and spice (both botanicals)

## BATCH SCALING

### Small Batch (10 drinks)
- 450ml aged rum
- 150ml honey liqueur
- 10 tsp mustard seeds (separate)
- 30 drops nutmeg essence
Store spirits mixed, add seeds per drink

### Large Batch (50 drinks)
- 2.25L aged rum
- 750ml honey liqueur
- 50 tsp mustard seeds (separate)
- 150 drops nutmeg essence
Store components separately
