Claude I have my own recipe for making Vodka. I get 8Kg of Sugar and 1 pack of Turbo Yeast Still Spirits Classic and 25 L of Water and mix it all together. I do a 50L batch and add two packets of Wash Carbon from Still Spirits and 1 bag of Home Brand Clumping Kitty Litter which is Bentonite about 4Kgs. The carbon and Bentonite cleanse the alcohol as it ferments. I start the mix at about 45 degrees and just forget about it for two weeks. After two weeks I filter the mixture through 10 Kg of Zeolite which is pool filter mix, this removes some water and acts as an Ion stripper. After this I leave the mixture to settle for an hour and all the contaminants have settled to the bottom. After this I filter the mixture through two Activated charcoal water filters of 1 micron and 0.1 micron into the still. I distill with a very high water velocity going through the still, I recycle the water, it doesnt matter if it gets hot. I chuck out the first 200mLs of distillate and I never collect the distillate over 82 degrees celcius. After this I water the mixture down to 40% alchohol and suspend a sock of activated charcoal in it and leave it for a week. Wash the charcoal first in water. Can you put this all in an artifact.
