# COCKTAIL MENU SYSTEM

## SIGNATURE COCKTAILS

### Coastal Series (Using Coastal Essence Base)

1. Saltbush Martini
   - Coastal Gin (2oz)
   - Dry vermouth (0.5oz)
   - Coastal Essence tincture (2 drops)
   - Garnish: Native herb air

2. Sea Spray Fizz
   - Bush Vodka (1.5oz)
   - Coastal Essence syrup (0.75oz)
   - Lime juice (0.5oz)
   - Soda top
   - Garnish: Sea parsley foam

3. Maritime Mule
   - Spiced Rum (2oz)
   - Coastal Gin (0.5oz)
   - Ginger beer
   - Coastal tincture (3 drops)
   - Garnish: Crystallized saltbush

### Bush Tucker Series (Using Bush Orchard Base)

1. Native Berry Sour
   - Bush Vodka (2oz)
   - Bush Orchard syrup (1oz)
   - Lemon juice (0.75oz)
   - Aquafaba
   - Garnish: Dehydrated fruit blend

2. Riberry Rickey
   - Coastal Gin (2oz)
   - Bush Orchard cordial (1oz)
   - Lime juice (0.5oz)
   - Soda
   - Garnish: Fresh riberry

3. Bush Manhattan
   - Rye whiskey (2oz)
   - Bush Orchard bitters (2 dashes)
   - Sweet vermouth (1oz)
   - Garnish: Muntries skewer

### Spice Trail Series (Using Outback Heat Base)

1. Pepperleaf Old Fashioned
   - Bourbon (2oz)
   - Spiced Forest syrup (0.3oz)
   - Outback Heat tincture (2 drops)
   - Garnish: Native pepper rim

2. Aniseed Highball
   - Spiced Rum (1.5oz)
   - Aniseed myrtle soda
   - Outback Heat bitters
   - Garnish: Lemon myrtle leaf

3. Desert Negroni
   - Coastal Gin (1oz)
   - Campari (1oz)
   - Sweet vermouth (1oz)
   - Outback Heat tincture
   - Garnish: Burnt strawberry gum

### Forest Floor Series (Using Native Forest Base)

1. Macadamia Mai Tai
   - Spiced Rum (2oz)
   - Native orgeat (0.75oz)
   - Lime juice (0.75oz)
   - Triple sec (0.5oz)
   - Garnish: Candied nut mix

2. Bunya Flip
   - Cognac (1.5oz)
   - Native Forest syrup (0.5oz)
   - Whole egg
   - Nutmeg
   - Garnish: Grated bunya nut

3. Wattleseed Espresso
   - Bush Vodka (1.5oz)
   - Coffee liqueur (1oz)
   - Wattleseed extract (0.25oz)
   - Garnish: Three coffee beans

## SEASONAL ROTATIONS

### Summer Variants
- Increase citrus portions
- Add crushed ice options
- Introduce spritz variations
- Lighter spirit portions

### Winter Variants
- Increase spirit portions
- Add hot drink options
- More nut-based drinks
- Reduced citrus
