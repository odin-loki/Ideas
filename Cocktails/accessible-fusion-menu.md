# ACCESSIBLE AUSTRALIAN FUSION COCKTAILS

## NATIVE FRUIT EXPRESSIONS

### Riberry Shochu Dreams
*Japan meets Queensland*
- Base: Shochu
- Key Ingredients:
  - Riberry syrup
  - Umeshu (Japanese plum wine)
  - Native mint tincture
  - Shiso leaf
- Method: Wet shake, fine strain
- Garnish: Fresh riberry and shiso leaf
- Profile: Tart, floral, with clove undertones

### Rosella Pisco Punch
*Peru meets Northern Australia*
- Base: Pisco
- Key Ingredients:
  - Wild rosella syrup
  - Chicha morada (Purple corn drink)
  - Native raspberry essence
  - Bush lime
- Method: Build and roll
- Garnish: Whole preserved rosella flower
- Profile: Deep purple, fruity, complex

## PREPARATION TECHNIQUES

### Native Fruit Syrups
Riberry Syrup:
1. Crush fresh riberries
2. Equal parts sugar and water
3. Simmer 15 minutes
4. Strain and bottle
5. Add 10ml vodka per 500ml for preservation

Rosella Syrup:
1. Steep dried rosellas in hot water
2. Add equal parts sugar
3. Reduce to desired consistency
4. Strain through muslin
5. Bottle and refrigerate

### Nut Preparations
Macadamia Orgeat:
1. Toast macadamias at 150°C for 15 minutes
2. Blend with water until fine
3. Strain through nut milk bag
4. Add sugar and orange blossom water
5. Add brandy for preservation

Bunya Nut Cream:
1. Shell and boil bunya nuts
2. Blend with equal parts cream
3. Add vanilla and sugar
4. Strain through fine mesh
5. Refrigerate, use within 5 days

### Herb Infusions
River Mint Infusion:
1. Bruise fresh river mint
2. Cover with cold vodka
3. Infuse for 24 hours
4. Strain and bottle
5. Store in dark bottle

Saltbush Tincture:
1. Dehydrate saltbush leaves
2. Cover with high-proof neutral spirit
3. Infuse for 5 days
4. Strain and dilute to taste
5. Store in dropper bottle

## SOURCING GUIDE

### Fruit Sources
- Riberries: North QLD suppliers, December-February
- Muntries: South Australian markets, January-March
- Native raspberries: Tasmania, summer months
- Rosella: Northern Territory, dry season

### Herb Sources
- River mint: Victorian waterways
- Native thyme: Tasmania
- Saltbush: Arid region suppliers
- Sea parsley: Coastal foragers

### Nut Sources
- Macadamias: Northern NSW/QLD farms
- Bunya nuts: QLD specialists, seasonal
- Native almonds: SA/VIC suppliers

## SERVICE NOTES

### Seasonal Adaptations
Summer:
- Fresh fruit forward
- Light, bright herbs
- Crushed ice service
- Minimal cream elements

Winter:
- Preserved fruits
- Warming spices
- Hot cocktail options
- Nut-forward drinks

### Sustainability Practices
- Work with local foragers
- Support indigenous suppliers
- Use whole ingredients
- Preserve seasonal surplus
- Minimize waste through syrup and tincture production

### Storage Guidelines
Syrups:
- Refrigerate all fruit syrups
- Use within 2 weeks
- Check daily for fermentation

Infusions:
- Store in dark bottles
- Label with date and batch
- Test weekly for flavor consistency

Nuts:
- Vacuum seal unused portions
- Refrigerate all preparations
- Date all batches
- Regular quality checks
