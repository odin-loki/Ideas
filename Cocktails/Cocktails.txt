Black Pepper Mary

2 oz vodka
4 oz tomato juice
1⁄2 oz peppercorn brine
1⁄4 oz lemon juice
2 dashes Worcestershire sauce
Fresh cracked black pepper and a lemon wedge for garnish
Shake all ingredients with ice, strain into a tall glass over fresh ice, and garnish.

Peppercorn Martini

2 oz gin
1⁄4 oz dry vermouth
1⁄4 oz peppercorn brine
2 whole black peppercorns for garnish
Stir with ice until well-chilled, strain into a chilled martini glass, and garnish.

Spiced Mule

2 oz bourbon
3⁄4 oz peppercorn brine
4 oz ginger beer
1⁄4 oz lime juice
Candied ginger and fresh cracked pepper for garnish
Build in a copper mug over ice, stir gently to combine, and garnish.

Smoked Pepper Manhattan

2 oz rye whiskey (or a smoky Scotch if you prefer)
3/4 oz sweet vermouth
1/4 oz peppercorn brine
2 dashes Angostura bitters
Garnish: Cocktail cherry and a few whole peppercorns
Stir with ice, strain into a chilled coupe glass, and garnish.

Black Smoke Old Fashioned

2 oz peated Scotch (like Laphroaig or Ardbeg)
1/4 oz peppercorn brine
1 bar spoon demerara syrup
2 dashes orange bitters
Garnish: Orange twist and fresh cracked black pepper
Build in a rocks glass with a large ice cube, stir until well-chilled, and garnish.

Peppered Boulevardier

1.5 oz bourbon
1 oz Campari
1 oz sweet vermouth
1/4 oz peppercorn brine
Garnish: Orange peel studded with peppercorns
Stir with ice, strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice, and garnish.

Forest Floor Manhattan

2 oz rye whiskey
3/4 oz sweet vermouth
1/2 oz mushroom-butter stock
2 dashes black walnut bitters
Garnish: Dried mushroom chip and thyme sprig
Stir with ice, strain into a chilled coupe glass, and garnish.

Woodland Martini

2 oz gin (something earthy like Hendrick's)
1/2 oz dry vermouth
1/4 oz mushroom-butter stock
Garnish: Butter-sautéed mushroom on a pick
Stir ingredients with ice until very cold, strain into a chilled martini glass, and garnish.

Umami Mary (a savory twist on a Bloody Mary)

2 oz vodka
3 oz tomato juice
1 oz mushroom-butter stock
Dash of soy sauce
Fresh black pepper
Garnish: Butter-sautéed mushroom and cherry tomato skewer
Shake with ice, strain into a tall glass over fresh ice, garnish.

Nordic Maritime

2 oz aquavit
1/4 oz salmon oil (just a few drops, really)
3/4 oz dry vermouth
2 dashes dill bitters (if available)
Garnish: Fresh dill sprig and lemon twist
Gently stir with ice, being careful not to aggressively shake the oil. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.

Fisherman's Breakfast (a unique take on a Bloody Mary)

2 oz vodka
4 oz tomato juice
3 drops salmon oil
1/2 oz lemon juice
Fresh horseradish
Fresh cracked black pepper
Garnish: Cucumber slice and fresh herbs
Build in glass over ice, stir gently to incorporate.

Ocean Mist

2 oz gin
1/2 oz dry vermouth
3 drops salmon oil
Small pinch of sea salt
Garnish: Preserved lemon
Stir with ice, strain into a chilled glass.

Toasted Grain Fizz

2 oz wheat beer reduction (wheat beer simmered down to concentrate)
1.5 oz bourbon
1/2 oz honey syrup
1/4 oz lemon juice
Top with prosecco
Garnish: Toasted fennel seed
Shake first four ingredients, strain into a flute, top with prosecco.

Sourdough Martini

2 oz vodka infused with toasted sourdough
1/2 oz dry vermouth
Barspoon of olive brine
Garnish: Olive and a small piece of toasted sourdough
Stir with ice, strain into chilled martini glass.

Baker's Old Fashioned

2 oz rye whiskey
1/4 oz malted barley syrup
2 dashes aromatic bitters
1 dash cereal bitters (if available)
Garnish: Orange peel and toasted caraway seed
Build in glass over large ice cube, stir until well-chilled.

Bacon, Cheese & Tomato

Bottom Layer (Bread Layer):

Toasted bread-infused vodka or bourbon
Touch of malted barley syrup for that wheat/yeast note
Could be slightly cloudy to mimic bread's appearance

Middle Layer (Cheese & Bacon):

Fat-washed vodka using bacon grease
For cheese flavor, we could use a touch of clarified whey or even incorporate a cheese-washed spirit
This layer should be more opaque and possibly creamy in texture

Top Layer (Tomato):

Clarified tomato juice
Touch of basil
Bright red and clear
Could float due to density differences
