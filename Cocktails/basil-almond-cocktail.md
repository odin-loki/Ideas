# BASIL & ALMOND COCKTAIL: "THE HERBALIST'S COMFORT"

## BASE RECIPE

### Core Specifications
- 45ml Premium Gin
- 30ml Fresh Almond Milk*
- 8-10 Fresh Basil Leaves
- 15ml Honey Syrup (3:1)
- 2 drops Almond Essence
- Coupe Glass
- No ice in final serve

*Barista-grade almond milk recommended

### Build Method
1. Add basil to shaker
2. Add honey syrup
3. Gentle muddle (3-4 presses)
4. Add gin
5. Add almond milk
6. Add almond essence
7. Dry shake first (10 seconds)
8. Shake with ice (12 seconds)
9. Double strain into chilled coupe

## COMPONENT PREPARATIONS

### Almond Milk Selection/Prep
Premium Options:
- Fresh-made almond milk
- Barista-grade commercial
- Must be unsweetened
Note: Test for splitting with alcohol

### Basil Preparation
- Use fresh sweet basil only
- Keep leaves whole
- Wash and dry thoroughly
- Gentle handling
- Clap before use

### Honey Syrup (3:1 ratio)
- 300g Raw honey
- 100ml Hot water
- Stir until integrated
- Bottle and chill
- Use within 2 weeks

## VARIATIONS

### Summer Fresh

- Served over ice

### Winter Comfort
- Warm almond milk

- Garnish with nutmeg

### Non-Alcoholic
- Replace gin with herb tea
- Increase almond essence
- Add orange blossom
- More honey syrup

## TECHNICAL NOTES

### Temperature Control
- Pre-chill coupe
- Shake hard with ice
- Serve immediately
- Milk must be cold

### Texture Management
1. Preventing Splitting:
   - Temperature control
   - Order of addition
   - Shake intensity
   - Quality of milk

2. Achieving Foam:
   - Dry shake first
   - Double strain
   - Fresh ingredients
   - Proper dilution

### Troubleshooting
If Too Sweet:
- Reduce honey
- More basil
- Less almond essence
- Add lemon twist

If Too Herbal:
- Reduce basil
- More honey
- More almond
- Less muddling
