---

## ⚙️ Overview

**Diffusion Welding** explores diffusion bonding processes applied to ultra-capacitor manufacturing and defence/aerospace applications. This work combines materials science with practical process engineering to enable high-quality diffusion-bonded joints.

### Key Concepts

- **Diffusion Bonding**: Solid-state joining through atomic diffusion
- **Ultra-Capacitors**: High-performance energy storage devices
- **Defence Transfer**: Military technology transfer pathways
- **Wartime Manufacturing**: Production under constrained conditions
